Brand

The Lift mark.

A U, cut in two. The left half is the drag: the slow, manual work that holds a business down. The right half is that same work after we've been through it. It has risen, and it's brighter.

Construction

Built from one idea.

01
Two concentric arcsAn outer radius of 36 and an inner of 16 give arms 20 units thick: heavy enough to read at 16px.
02
One rising lineBoth arm tops are cut along the same 2:5 slope, the blue dashed line.
03
One slice, square to itThe U is cut at right angles to the rising line, so every straight edge in the mark runs one of two ways.
04
The liftThe right half rises 16 units (4/9 of the outer radius) from where it sat, dashed, and turns from deep blue to mint as it goes.

Usage

Give it room to rise.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of one arm of the mark. Don't use the full logo smaller than 96px wide, or the mark smaller than 16px tall; below that, use the favicon version, which has a wider gap.

Don't rotate or tilt it.

Don't swap the colours. The lift is always the bright half.

Don't close the gap or level the halves.

Don't stretch, squash or add effects.

Colour

Paper, ink, and flat colour.

The site is printed, not lit: near-neutral paper and ink with flat colour. The paper carries only a trace of warmth, so the blue stays crisp and the mint stays clean. Blue is the drag, mint is the lift, and windsock orange is kept for anything you can press. The logo keeps its own gradients, listed below.
