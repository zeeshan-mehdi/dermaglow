The problem

Everyone wants AI.
Few know where it fits.

Most teams aren't short on ambition — they're short on a clear map. Years of documents, spreadsheets and tickets sit unused. Hours vanish into copy-paste work. And "let's add AI" turns into a chatbot nobody asked for. We start somewhere else: with how your work really flows.

FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

We don't know where AI fits in our business. Is that okay?

That's exactly where most of our clients start. Our bottleneck audit exists to answer that question with evidence — sometimes the best fix is AI, sometimes it's a simple automation or a better tool.

We have a lot of data, but it's messy. Can you work with it?

Yes. Most valuable data is messy — scanned PDFs, old spreadsheets, email threads. We clean, structure and connect it so AI systems can search, reason over and act on it safely.

Do you only do AI?

No. We're a full-service studio: software engineering, design, DevOps, marketing and consulting. AI is our specialty, but we'll help with whatever moves your business forward.

How long does an engagement last?

As long as it's useful. A bottleneck sprint takes 2–3 weeks; a build usually takes weeks to a few months; many clients keep us on afterwards to run and improve what we built.

What about our data privacy and security?

We work under NDA, follow least-privilege access, and design every system around your compliance needs — including private model deployments when data can't leave your environment.

Do you work with startups or only larger companies?

Both. Startups come to us to build and launch fast; established companies come to modernise operations. The process scales to either.
