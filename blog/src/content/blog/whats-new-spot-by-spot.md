This is the biggest update Dermaglow has had. It looks different, it sees
more, and the coach finally knows what you know: which spots are new,
which ones have been there for weeks, and which have quietly gone.

It's rolling out on the App Store now, and the same version is in testing
on Android.

<span class="section-num">01</span>

## A new look

Dermaglow has a new face, literally. The icon is a single stroke of a smile
with a soft glow, and the whole app moved to match: light, calm, and blue.
Every screen was redrawn, from the Today page to the coach, with clearer
type and fewer things competing for your attention.

## Every spot, mapped and followed

A scan now places each spot exactly where it is on your face, on your own
photo or on a clean face map, whichever you prefer. Then it does the part
that actually matters: it follows each spot to the next scan.

So instead of a fresh count every time, you see a story. **New** since last
time. **Still there**, and since when. **Cleared**. A spot that has lasted
three scans says so, with the date it was first seen.

A few details we cared about:

- **Faint spots are kept apart.** If the scan isn't sure, it lists the spot
  as *possible* rather than counting it. If it's real, the next scan will
  confirm it.
- **Lines, creases, freckles and shine aren't spots.** We worked hard on
  this. A freckle is a freckle, not a blemish.
- **More of your face is covered.** The scan now reads the whole forehead,
  and the side photos let it see along your jaw and cheeks.
- **The under-eye area gets noted too,** whether it's dark circles,
  puffiness or hollows, with which one it looks like.
- **Capture is steadier.** The camera waits for you to be still and sharp
  before it takes each photo, and takes them at a higher resolution.

## A coach that knows your spots

The coach now sees the same spot history you do. Ask "is the spot on my
chin new?" and it can tell you it's been there for two weeks, or that it
showed up since your last scan. Ask "are my breakouts clearing?" and the
answer comes from your scans, not from general advice.

It's also better at doing things, not just talking about them:

- **Weekly care works properly.** Ask for a mask once a week, or to make
  your exfoliant a twice-a-week step, and it lands in your Weekly tab, not
  in your daily list.
- **It knows your main goal,** and shapes its advice around it.
- **It's quicker.** Replies come back noticeably faster, especially when it
  is changing your routine or adding several products at once.

As always, nothing changes in your account until you tap to accept it.

<span class="section-num">04</span>

## Your goal, week by week

Tap your goal on the Today screen and you'll see what a steady routine
usually does for it, in four stretches: weeks 1–2, 3–4, 5–8 and 9–12 and
beyond. Dermaglow marks where you are, counted from your first scan.

We wrote these timelines to be honest rather than exciting. Pores, for
example, are mostly genetic in size; what a routine changes is how clear,
and so how visible, they are. Where it makes sense, the timeline also says
when it's worth seeing a dermatologist.

You can change your goal right from that page, or ask the coach whether
your routine is set up for it.

## The smaller things

- **Reminders arrive.** Routine and diary reminders are now set up
  automatically for your routines, including new ones, without needing to
  visit Settings first.
- **"Your scan is ready."** Switch to another app while a scan is being
  read, and you'll get a notification when the result is in.
- **A tidier Settings page.** Everything personal, like your skin profile,
  routines, products and plan, lives on your profile. Settings is now just
  settings.
- **Allergy checks are stricter.** Ingredients you're allergic to are
  caught under their other names too.
- **The scan report you can share** now lists possible spots separately,
  so a doctor or friend sees exactly what was certain and what wasn't.

<span class="section-num">06</span>

## What hasn't changed

Your photos stay private and are never sold or used for advertising. You
can delete everything from Settings at any time. And Dermaglow is still a
guide, not a diagnosis: it points you to a dermatologist whenever something
deserves a closer look.
